Introduction

What DT Medix is, and who builds it

DT Medix is diagnostic center, pathology lab and clinic management software. It follows a patient through the real flow of a lab, from registration and appointment to the invoice, the barcode sample, the lab result and the report, and it also runs the staff side with attendance, leave and payroll.

Overview

One connected system for the whole diagnostic center, not just the billing counter

Most diagnostic center software stops at invoices. DT Medix follows the patient through the real flow of a lab: registration, doctor appointment, test order, invoice and money receipt, sample collection with a barcode label, receiving in the lab, result entry with automatic abnormal flags, critical value acknowledgement and report templates for pathology and imaging.

It is also built to run the business around the lab. Owners get multi-branch control, role-based access, discount and refund approvals and an access log of every patient record view. HR teams get employees, shifts, leave, attendance from ZKTeco fingerprint devices or manual entry, and monthly payroll with printable payslips.

Every center gets its own installation and database, so patient data is never mixed with another business. The whole interface works in English and Bangla, with Bangla names and receipts, and it is designed for fast keyboard-first work at the reception and lab counters.

Why centers need it

Diagnostic centers lose time, money and trust in places the owner cannot see

Paper registers, spreadsheets and a separate billing program can run a small lab, until the first mix-up, missed due or late report.

Sample mix-ups

Hand-written labels and loose tubes cause wrong-patient results. Barcode labels printed at collection remove the guesswork.

Duplicate patients

The same patient registered twice splits history and billing. Duplicate warnings on mobile and name keep one clean record.

Late and lost reports

Without a worklist nobody sees which test is overdue. Turnaround countdowns and urgent pinning show what needs attention first.

Dues and discounts out of control

Verbal discounts and forgotten balances leak revenue. Approval limits, reasons and a due list keep every taka accounted for.

Critical values missed

A dangerous result must reach the doctor. DT Medix blocks the result until someone records who was informed and when.

Staff time and salary guesswork

Attendance on paper and salary in a spreadsheet invite disputes. Device punches feed payroll so the month-end sheet is ready.

Workflow

From the front desk to the finished report

Every step uses the same patient, order and sample records, so nothing is typed twice and every handover is visible.

1Register

Create or find the patient; duplicate warning on mobile and name.

2Book

Pick a doctor slot and issue a queue token, or skip for walk-ins.

3Order

Add tests and packages at the reception counter, apply approved discounts.

4Pay

Take cash, partial payment or leave a due; print invoice and receipt.

5Collect

Print barcode labels, collect the sample and record the collector.

6Receive

Lab scans the label, receives or rejects the sample with a reason.

7Result

Enter results; flags and critical alerts are calculated for you.

8Report

Use a table or narrative template; view the finished result.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us

What is DT Medix?

DT Medix is diagnostic center, pathology lab and clinic management software by Datatech BD Ltd. It connects patient registration, appointments, test orders, billing, barcode sample tracking, lab result entry, staff attendance and payroll in one system for Bangladesh.

Who should use diagnostic center management software?

Any diagnostic center, pathology lab, clinic or collection center that bills tests, collects samples and wants fewer mistakes, faster counters and clear numbers. DT Medix suits one counter and multi-branch groups.

Is DT Medix a pathology lab software with barcode sample tracking?

Yes. Every sample gets a unique ID and a Code 128 barcode label. You can collect, receive, reject with a reason and recollect samples, and scan a label to open the sample in the lab.

How does DT Medix flag abnormal and critical lab results?

Each test parameter has reference ranges by age and sex and optional critical limits. When you enter a result, the system flags it as normal, high, low or critical and stores the range used. A critical value needs an acknowledgement before it can move on.

Does it work for multiple branches?

Yes. Run many branches in one installation with a branch switcher, branch-wise staff, optional branch price lists and strict branch isolation. Branch staff see only their branch, and owners see all.

Is the software available in Bangla?

Yes. The whole interface, receipts and message templates work in English and Bangla, patient names can be saved in Bangla, and you can switch language in one click.

Can I manage doctor appointments and tokens?

Yes. Set weekly schedules and leave days per doctor, book free slots, issue token numbers, follow the booked, arrived, in consultation and completed flow, and let patients book on your public website.

FAQ (continued)

Questions owners ask us

Does DT Medix support ZKTeco fingerprint attendance and payroll?

Yes. ZKTeco devices can push punches to DT Medix using the ADMS protocol, or you can import the device file. You can also mark attendance by hand. Late minutes, overtime, leave and holidays feed a monthly payroll with printable payslips.

How are discounts, refunds and dues controlled?

Each center sets a discount limit. Larger discounts, refunds and cancellations after collection need a manager approval and a reason. Partial payments are supported, and the due balance is always calculated, never typed.

Is patient data safe, and do you send results by SMS?

Each center has its own installation and database. Branch data is isolated, every record view is logged, and message templates never contain results or diagnoses. Report delivery is designed around short notices and secure links.

Does it need internet, and does it work on tablets and phones?

DT Medix runs in a web browser and needs an internet connection. It works on counter PCs, tablets and phones, and warns you when the connection drops so a form is never lost silently.

Which features are ready today and which are coming?

Ready today: patients, appointments, billing, test catalog, samples, lab results, report templates, approvals, branches, roles, attendance, leave and payroll. On the roadmap: signed PDF reports with QR checking, SMS and WhatsApp, mobile payments, doctor commission, the owner dashboard and the patient portal.
