# Reel 01: Bangla hook, fast conversion (30 seconds, 9:16)

Goal: get a diagnostic center or clinic owner to open the free demo or call/WhatsApp.
Audience: owners and managers of diagnostic centers, pathology labs and clinics in Bangladesh.
Tone: fast, direct, spoken Bangla. Voice-over plus bold on-screen text. No stock-photo feel; use real screens from `docs/graphics/screenshots/v1`.

## Hook options (pick one, test the others)

1. "আপনার ডায়াগনস্টিক সেন্টারে কি এখনও খাতায় রিপোর্ট আর হিসাব চলছে?"
2. "ভুল রোগীর রিপোর্ট চলে গেলে কী হবে, ভেবেছেন?"
3. "প্রতি মাসে কত টাকা বকেয়া আর ডিসকাউন্টে হারাচ্ছেন, জানেন?"

## Production notes

- Format 1080x1920, safe area: keep text inside the middle 80 percent so Reels buttons do not cover it.
- Cut every 2 to 4 seconds; add a quick zoom or highlight ring on the key button in each screen.
- Voice-over: clear, energetic male or female Bangla voice; add Bangla captions burned in for silent viewers.
- Music: upbeat, no vocals, lowered under the voice-over.
- Keep claims to built features (see the product book); do not promise results such as exact savings.
- Pinned comment: demo link and phone number.
